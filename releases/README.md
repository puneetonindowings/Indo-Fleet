### What Was Fixed & Added in v1.1.0:
- **Dedicated Full-Screen Airframe Inspection:** Converted post-delivery/scan modal into an immersive full-screen inspection dashboard with 700 RPAV compliance checklists.
- **Photo & Video Evidence Capture:** Directly snap photos or record up to 30s video clips of rotors, payload bay, and airframe with live thumbnails and gallery attachment.
- **Real Hardware Battery & GPS Telemetry:** Directly queries actual battery level sensors and GPS fix on SOS emergency transmission.
- **Cleaned Dashboard Metrics:** Removed redundant 'In Queue' and 'Missed' cards; unified around the 4 core metrics: Total Delivered, Today's Delivery, Assigned, and In Transit.
- **Secure Manual Acceptance OTP:** Eliminated client auto-fill in acceptance modals, requiring true email verification codes.
- **Admin Emergency SOS Active Sorting:** Automatically prioritizes active SOS beacons with audible dispatches and real-time map telemetry.
